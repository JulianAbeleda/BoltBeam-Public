# Replay workload: the 27B hybrid on the RTX 5090, 2026-10-11

A replay plays a recorded session through the user's own llama-server command and shows, per turn and for the
session, where the time goes against the roofline. It shows where. It does not say why.

## What was built

- `boltbeam.replay_trace.v1` (`boltbeam/trace/replay_trace.py`). A session as counts per turn: new tokens, tool
  tokens, reply tokens, a compaction flag, and the system prompt's size. It never holds chat text. `check()` refuses
  any string over 80 characters outside a `words` field.
- Two readers. `shape` draws counts from `boltbeam/data/replay_shapes.json` with a seed. It ships `agent-60` (about
  131K tokens) and `chat-300` (about 105K tokens, shaped like a GameTerm session). `gameterm` counts a GameTerm
  transcript and its save with the engine's tokenizer. Only counts and the file's sha256 leave the reader. The system
  prompt's size is stated by the caller, because the logs do not hold it.
- The replay (`boltbeam/collectors/replay.py`). It starts the user's command with only `--host` and `--port`
  replaced. It plays each turn through `/v1/chat/completions` with the user's template and the prompt cache on. Each
  reply is greedy and exactly the trace's length. Per turn it records the server's `cache_n`, `prompt_n`, `prompt_ms`,
  `predicted_n` and `predicted_ms`, the client's send, first-token and last-token times, and the GPU clock at 10 ms.
- The server's settings come from the user's command, else llama.cpp's defaults, and from its log (context,
  checkpoints, prompt cache). Each setting records its source. The default log level does not print the chunk or the
  cache types.
- Per-turn ceilings (`boltbeam/workflow/replay.py`, `prefill_work.turn_ceiling`). Prefill of the tokens the turn
  needed. Prefill of what the server did (the difference is recompute). Decode at the turn's prefix: the ledger, the
  KV cache and the recurrent state in and out. Peaks are scaled to the turn's SM clock. Bandwidth is not scaled.
- Session efficiency `E` = sum of the warm turns' rooflines over their time. The cold first turn is a row. It is left
  out of the headline.
- Step 5 captures four turns with llama-bench at the turn's depth with the server's flags. A capture counts only when
  llama-bench's measured repetition is within 10% of the server's `prompt_ms`. Otherwise the turn is `unmeasured`.
  One decode capture runs at the deepest turn.
- Output: the text summary, an HTML section, and the TUI. The TUI's Workload page has a replay row, a shape picker,
  and a server picker. The server picker lists the running llama-server commands. A command is used only after the
  user confirms it.
- `replay-diff` compares two replays of one trace turn by turn (`boltbeam.replay_diff.v1`). It is refused when the
  trace, the model or the target differ.

## Runs

- Model: the 27B hybrid, Q4_K_M.
- Server: the user's own command. Context 262,144 tokens, q8_0 K and V cache, one slot, the user's chat template.
- Chip: RTX 5090 with the fixed power policy.
- One run per trace. agent-60 ran with captures. chat-300 ran without them.
- chat-300 read the chip's registry bandwidth (1,693.3 GB/s). agent-60 read the bandwidth measured on this GPU

## Findings (where, not why)

- Decode is most of a session: 80% to 85% of the time. It is also most of the loss. On agent-60, decode above its
  ceiling loses 152 s and prefill loses 60 s.
- Every turn recomputes some tokens it had cached: 67 to 795 per turn. The server reuses less than the prompt plus
  the reply it made. Recompute costs 4 s to 6 s per session.
- The time outside `prompt_ms` grows with depth on chat-300. It is 25 ms at turn 2, 233 ms at 56K and 462 ms at
  108K. Short turns at depth spend more of their TTFT there than in the prompt. This settles scope risk 1 as a
  where: that time is outside the server's `prompt_ms`, so the replay counts it as overhead. A trace of the live
  server would be needed to name the part.
- Captured turns (agent-60):
  - Turn 2 (early) is `unmeasured`. llama-bench took 594.8 ms. The server took 704.6 ms.
  - Turns 31, 60 and 56 tie out. The server's prompt path is 111 ms to 180 ms above the bench's.
  - In those turns attention is the largest row. At 128K deep it takes 1,305 ms against a 366 ms ceiling, at 28% MFU.
  - `mul_mat_q` (383 ms to 524 ms) has no ceiling. No role could be paired with its launches.
  - The recurrent scan runs at about 6% MFU against its fp32 ceiling.
- Decode at 130,851 tokens: the kernels take 19.9 ms per token against a 13.65 ms limit. The server's own token
  takes 20.6 ms. The FFN gate and up roles were not paired in that capture, so their time is in the "no role" row.

## Open

- The `mul_mat_q` launches in the deep prefill captures and the FFN gate and up roles in the deep decode capture are
  not paired with roles. Their time is real. Their ceilings are not known.
- The early turn's capture is `unmeasured`: it differs from the server by 16%.
- The `gameterm` reader needs the system prompt's size. A GameTerm change that logs `usage` and `cache_n` per turn
  would remove that input. That change needs its own packet.
- vLLM and Ollama replay adapters are not built. Apple telemetry and peaks are not built.
- The diff has a JSON output and a text output. It has no HTML section and no TUI page yet.
